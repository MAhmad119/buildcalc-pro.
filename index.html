REFERENCE & METHOD

How BuildCalc Pro Works

Understand the assumptions behind your estimates.

Concrete

Uses a 1.54 dry-volume factor and selected nominal cement:sand:aggregate volume ratio.

Mortar

Uses a simplified dry-volume factor and the selected cement:sand ratio.

Brickwork

Uses an approximate planning allowance of 500 bricks per cubic metre of masonry.

Finishing

Plaster, paint and tiles use entered thickness, surface coverage and wastage assumptions.

Do not use this website to size structural foundations, columns, beams, slabs or reinforcement. Always obtain site-specific professional design and verify quantities before purchasing materials.
